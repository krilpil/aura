# Project Overview

Aura is a public-facing website for a premium event venue near Arzamas, set in a forest by a calm river. The project should communicate the idea of a refined city-level restaurant and banquet service placed in nature: weddings, anniversaries, graduations, gender reveal parties, corporate events, outdoor ceremonies, riverside receptions, and private celebrations.

The core promise is a no-compromise mix of atmosphere and service: forest, river, fresh air, scenic views, and privacy, combined with designer interiors, cleanliness, comfort, premium service, strong event organization, and a high-end food experience.

Key venue facts agents should preserve in product copy and UI decisions:

- Two event halls: "Лесная симфония" for up to 60 guests and "Большая вода" for up to 120 guests.
- The brand tone is premium, atmospheric, warm, and confident, not rustic or picnic-like.
- The location is positioned as a top new event venue near Arzamas and in the Nizhny Novgorod region.
- Food is a major differentiator: fusion menu, chef-driven dishes, Russian ingredients with Asian techniques and European presentation.
- Catering is available for riverside ceremonies, forest clearings, receptions, mobile bar/service stations, white textiles, polished glassware, and outdoor banquet setups.
- Suitable events include dream weddings, anniversaries and birthdays, graduations, gender reveal parties, and corporate events with projector and sound support.
- The main call to action is booking dates for the summer season, especially Fridays and Saturdays.
- Current promotional offer: when booking before the end of the month, guests receive an edible-format appetizer board / spread tasting for the reception as a gift.

When implementing UI or writing content, make the first impression feel like a premium forest-and-river event venue with restaurant-level service. Avoid making the project look like a campsite, casual picnic area, or generic restaurant landing page.
